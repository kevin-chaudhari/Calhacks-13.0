# Rally concierge

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3) ![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

**Find people for a group trip and get one negotiated plan, with the group discount.**

Tell Rally what you want to do, for example *"Scuba in Monterey next month under $300, I'm Open Water"*, and the concierge:

1. Compiles your goal (activity, place, dates, budget, group size) and asks if something is missing.
2. Finds people with the same goal who pass every hard filter, and tells you who didn't fit and why.
3. Has everyone's personal agents negotiate one shared plan with the vendors' agents (private preferences never leave each person's agent).
4. Replies with the agreed plan, the price per person, and a link to the live group room to approve.

Activities today: **scuba diving, skydiving, sea kayaking** (Monterey Bay and Santa Cruz).

Keywords: group trips, scuba, skydiving, kayaking, find people, group discount, group booking, travel buddies, trip planning.

Protocol: AgentChatProtocol (ChatMessage / ChatAcknowledgement). Vendors and quotes are fictional and labelled simulated.
