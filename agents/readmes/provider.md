# Rally provider agent

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3) ![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

A vendor's agent in Rally. Answers group quote requests (`QuoteRequestWire` → `QuoteWire`) from the vendor's price tiers: bigger groups get a lower per-person price. Vendors can require a staff member to approve the binding quote before it goes out.

Vendors and prices in this demo are fictional and labelled "Simulated".
